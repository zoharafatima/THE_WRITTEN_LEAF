 🍂 The Written Leaf

> **Where every book leaves a trace.**

**The Written Leaf** is a reading journal and community web application designed to help readers preserve their reading journey — not just the books they read, but the thoughts, experiences, and memories those books leave behind.

The idea is simple: every book becomes a part of a reader's story.

---

## 📖 About the Project

Traditional reading journals often require maintaining notebooks or documents, which can become difficult to keep up with over time.

The Written Leaf aims to make recording a reading journey more personal, visual, and enjoyable.

For each book, readers will be able to record:

* 📕 Front cover
* 📗 Back cover
* 📅 Start date
* 📅 Finish date
* ✍️ Personal experience and reflections
* 🔒 Privacy settings
* 🤝 Lending availability

Readers will be able to decide whether their reflections remain **private** or are shared publicly with the community.

The goal is not to create another platform focused solely on ratings and reviews, but to create a space where readers can **look back at who they were, what they read, and what those books left behind.**

---

## 🌱 Vision

The Written Leaf is being developed with three ideas at its heart:

**Read. Reflect. Remember.**

A reader should be able to return months or years later and see their reading journey unfold — where it started, what they read along the way, and how their thoughts evolved.

At the same time, readers who choose to share their experiences publicly can become part of a wider community.

## ✨ Planned Features

### 👤 Personal Reading Journey

* User accounts and profiles
* Personal bookshelf
* Reading history
* Currently reading / completed books
* Start and finish dates
* Personal reflections

### 🔐 Privacy

* Public or private book entries
* Private reflections visible only to their owner
* User-owned reading data

### 🌍 Reader Community

* Discover public reading journeys
* View other readers' public books
* Read personal reflections
* Like posts
* No comments, keeping the platform quieter and more personal

### 🤝 Book Lending

* Mark physical books as available for lending
* Send borrowing requests
* Accept or decline requests
* Communication between readers regarding lending

### 🎨 Design

The visual direction of The Written Leaf is inspired by:

**Victorian literary elegance + modern minimalism**

The aim is to make the application feel more like a beautiful personal library or journal than a conventional social media platform.

## 🔒 Privacy Philosophy

Privacy is a fundamental part of The Written Leaf.

Readers should never feel pressured to publish their thoughts.

Every reader decides what they want to share and what they want to keep for themselves.

A public reflection is something a reader **chooses** to share — not something they are expected to share.

---

## 🚀 Project Goals

The Written Leaf is being built as both:

1. A personal reading journal
2. A community space for readers

The long-term goal is to create a platform where people can **record, revisit, discover, connect, and share** through their reading journeys.
